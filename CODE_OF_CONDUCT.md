# Code of conduct

Be direct, respectful, and evidence-led. Do not expose another person's private
career, immigration, identity, contact, or application information. Harassment,
discrimination, doxxing, and pressure to bypass provider or user consent are not
accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's
security/contact channel. The maintainer may remove content or participation that
puts people, data, or the project at risk.
