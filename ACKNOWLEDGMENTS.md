# Acknowledgments

Nimanto exists because maintainers make reliable open-source building blocks available to everyone.

We are especially grateful to the communities behind Node.js, TypeScript, Next.js, React, Fastify, PGlite, Vitest, Playwright, PDF.js, pdf-lib, docx, Lucide, Fontsource, and the CycloneDX tooling used to build, test, document, and inspect this beta.

The interface draws on Japanese restraint and modern Indian craft geometry as cultural direction. Its bilingual microtype and jaali-inspired ornament are original project work, not copied third-party assets.

The exact machine-readable dependency inventories for v0.1.0 are published as:

- [`docs/releases/nimanto-v0.1.0.cdx.json`](docs/releases/nimanto-v0.1.0.cdx.json) — CycloneDX 1.6 JSON;
- [`docs/releases/nimanto-v0.1.0.spdx.json`](docs/releases/nimanto-v0.1.0.spdx.json) — SPDX JSON.
